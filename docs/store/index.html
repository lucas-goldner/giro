01Fill these in first

  • Localizations: App Information › language menu (top right) › Add: 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Español (México / España), Italiano, Português (Brasil / Portugal). English (U.S.) is the primary. Spanish and Portuguese each get the same texts twice (Mexico and Spain; Brazil and Portugal).
  • Privacy Policy URL is required per localization and Giro has none published yet. Suggested home, next to Japanana's: https://apps-privacies-terms-lg.vercel.app/giro/privacy.html. One paragraph is enough: walks, spots and settings stay on the device; HealthKit workouts are read only to import walks and never leave the phone; location is used only while the app is open; map tiles are fetched from OpenFreeMap (which sees your IP address like any website); no accounts, no analytics, no ads.
  • Support URL: https://github.com/lucas-goldner/giro (or the privacy site).
  • HealthKit: the app uses the HealthKit entitlement, so App Review checks the purpose string and that Health data is not used for advertising. The App Review notes below say what it reads.
  • Export compliance: "Does your app use encryption?" → only HTTPS, exempt. Add ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO to Info.plist to skip the question on every build.
  • Screenshots: upload the 6.9" set for every language (section 05); it covers every iPhone. Giro is iPhone-only, so no iPad set.
  • Build: pick the latest Codemagic build (the build number comes from TestFlight + 1). Version string 1.1.0.

Description · English (U.S.)1188/4000
Walk, and the map comes to life.

Giro is a walking game for your city. Every street you walk is painted onto a 3D toy-town map. Everything you have not walked yet stays white clay. The more you explore, the more of the city appears.

EVERY WALK IS A STAGE
Record a walk with GO!, or import the ones your watch already saved. Each walk gets a rank from S to C for how much new ground it covered.

A CITY MADE OF TOYS
Pastel buildings with lit windows, trees in the parks and along the streets, and landmarks you will recognise: Tokyo Tower, the Skytree, torii at shrines, pagodas at temples. All of it white until you walk past.

SPOTS
Press and hold the map to mark a place. Walks that pass it collect it.

WORKS OFFLINE
Download your city once. The map is instant and works without a signal, and every walk is named after where it happened, straight from the offline map.

MADE FOR WALKERS
• Import every walk from Apple Health in one go
• Live timer, pace and a ring that fills with new ground
• Sliders for trail width and reveal radius
• In nine languages
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your walks stay on your phone.

06App Privacy

App Store Connect › App Privacy › Get started.

FieldValue
Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?No, we do not collect data from this app → label "Data Not Collected"
Why that is rightWalks, spots and settings are stored on the device (MMKV). HealthKit workouts are read to import walks and never leave the phone. Location is used only while recording. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no ads, no crash reporter. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap over HTTPS; its servers see the IP address like any web request, which Apple does not count as collection by the developer.

07App Review Information

App Store Connect › iOS App › 1.1.0 › App Review Information.

FieldValue
Sign-in requiredNo. There is no account.
ContactYour name, phone and email (the reviewer calls only if something is unclear).
Notes
Giro is a walking game: streets you walk are painted onto a 3D map, the rest stays white.

HealthKit: the app reads walking workouts and their routes only to import past walks as "stages" (Import › Load workouts / Import everything). Nothing is written to Health and no Health data leaves the device.

Location: used only while recording a walk (GO!), when-in-use authorization.

To see the app with data on a review device without walking: open Options (the slider icon on the home screen) › Demo data › "Fill Tokyo". This loads generated walks and spots across Tokyo; "Remove demo data" takes them away again. "Download map area" stores the map tiles for offline use (free OpenFreeMap tiles, no account).
