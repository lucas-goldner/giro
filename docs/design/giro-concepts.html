Giro concepts

Two directions for the fog-of-war walking app, four screens each: home, recording, walk detail and the walks list. The map is white until you walk it; every route uncovers the colour underneath. Sample data is three Tokyo walks and four saved places.

Sonic Generations

Game HUD energy: skewed italic condensed type with a drop outline, Sonic blue and navy with gold rings, diagonal speed stripes on panels, chevron-shaped buttons with a hard shadow, and ranks. Walks are "stages", places are "rings", the week's distance is the score. Uncovered map is pure white with an electric-blue glow around the route.

Zenly × Apple

Zenly's play (a warm gradient wash over the fog, a giant lowercase underlined place name, black rounded squares with gradient icons, one mint-to-pink pill) on Apple's structure (frosted glass sheets with a grabber, round icon tiles, hairline grouped lists, uppercase micro labels over big numbers). Colour is in exactly three places: the wash, the icons and the pill.

How to pick

  • Sonic turns the fog mechanic into a game loop (score, rank, rings, locked stages). It is the louder, more memorable one, and the harder one to keep clean as screens multiply; every new element needs a game metaphor.
  • Zenly × Apple keeps the map as the hero and the chrome native; the playfulness lives in the wash, the title and the icons. It is easier to extend and ages better, and it still looks like nothing else on the App Store.
  • Both share the same data model already in the app (routes, places, new-ground distance), so either can be built on the current Flutter screens without model changes.
